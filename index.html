The Photographer

Haru

Born in Hokkaido, Japan — raised on snow-capped mountains and wild coastlines. Haru picked up her first camera at age 14 to photograph the red-crowned cranes near her childhood home. She never stopped.

Today her work appears in National Geographic, BBC Wildlife, and Der Spiegel. She has permanent collections in galleries across Tokyo, London, and New York — and still sleeps in tents more nights than in hotels.
