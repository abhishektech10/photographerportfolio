Chapter One

The Story of Meeting You

Not every encounter leaves a mark, but some do — quietly, without announcement. The kind that only becomes clear in hindsight, when you realize a person changed something in the texture of your days.

Meeting you was one of those. Not dramatic. Not planned. Simply real — and because of that, entirely unforgettable.

There is a particular gratitude for people who show up authentically, who make space for genuine connection without effort or pretense. That is the rarest kind of person to meet.

✦

Some meetings are accidents. Some are gifts. Yours felt like both at once.
